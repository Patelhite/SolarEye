Bhai yehi to hamare project ka main concept hai. Predictive Maintenance ka matlab sensor khud nahi bolega "maintenance karo", balki system data analyze karke recommendation dega.

Example 1: Panel Dusty Ho Gaya
Light Intensity = 900 (bahut achhi sunlight)
Temperature = 32°C (normal)
Expected Power = 18W
Actual Power = 10W

System sochega:

"Sunlight to achhi hai, temperature bhi normal hai, phir power kam kyu aa rahi hai?"

Result:

⚠ Possible Solar Panel Cleaning Required
Example 2: Panel Overheating
Temperature = 60°C
Power Output continuously decreasing

Result:

⚠ Possible Solar Panel Overheating
⚠ Maintenance Recommended
Example 3: Wiring Issue
Voltage:
12V
11V
13V
8V
14V
7V

Bahut fluctuation.

Result:

⚠ Electrical Inspection Recommended
Hamare Project Mein Initially

INA219

hi hain.

To maintenance logic:

LDR → Sunlight check
INA219 → Voltage + Current
Power = V × I
DHT11 → Temperature

Fir:

High Light + Low Power
            ↓
Panel Dust / Cleaning Required

High Temp + Power Drop
            ↓
Overheating

Voltage Fluctuation
            ↓
Wiring Issue
